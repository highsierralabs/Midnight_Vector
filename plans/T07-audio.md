## 1. Purpose

After the first real gesture, the game has a synthesized synthwave soundtrack that never ends and ships no audio file. Observable outcomes:

1. **It plays what a synthwave track plays.** 100 BPM. Four-on-the-floor kick, a gated-reverb snare on 2 and 4, hats, a rolling detuned saw bass, a chorus-widened detuned pad that pumps against the kick, a plucked sixteenth arp through a dotted-eighth delay from SYNC 4, and a singing lead from SYNC 8.
2. **It evolves.** Each sector has its own chord progressions, melodies, bass pattern and arp pattern, in a key taken from `zone.musicKey`, reached through a tom fill, a riser and a crash on a phrase boundary. SYNC adds layers and brightness, and a 32-bar form gives it drive, lift, break and rebuild sections.
3. **The game plays the music.** Shards ring in the current chord on the sixteenth grid, rising with the chain. Gates stab the chord. A hit ducks the music by 9 dB through a 500 Hz lowpass for 400 ms. `SIGNAL_LOST` and the cruise menu muffle it.
4. **It behaves in a browser.** No `AudioContext` and no source before the first real gesture. No burst after a hidden tab. No drift against `sim.beat` above 30 ms. No clipping at full volume. An audio failure stays silent and never reaches the frame loop's fatal path (§3.3).

**No agent can hear this.** The objective gates in section 7 prove that the notes are correct, the right layers sound, the levels fit, the snare has a gated shape, and the timing holds. They do not prove that it sounds good. Section 7 ends with the **Human checks**, which need human ears, and T07 reports them as unverified.

## 2. Context and orientation

The working directory for every command is `C:\highsierralabs\terrain`. It is not a git repository. `§n` means a section of `architecture.md`. The design source is §7.6, the "Web Audio" section of `Agents.md`, and §10.4 and §10.5.

Terms used here:

- **Step:** a sixteenth note, 0.15 s. `step = floor(beat * 4)`. An integer-valued double, so never `|0` or `>>`.
- **Bar:** 16 steps, 2.4 s, 288 ticks, the period of `phases[1]`. **Phrase:** 4 bars, 9.6 s, the period of `phases[2]`. **Form cycle:** 32 bars, 76.8 s. A zone at cruise is 33.3 bars [arithmetic].
- **Sector:** `zone.index`. **Song:** `sector % 6`, which selects progressions and melodies. **Key:** `zone.musicKey`.
- **Tier:** a SYNC band. T0 is SYNC 1 to 3, T1 is 4 to 7, T2 is 8 to 11, T3 is 12 to 15, T4 is 16.
- **Virtual time:** an `OfflineAudioContext` suspended every 25 ms of audio time, so the production sequencer runs against `ctx.currentTime` exactly as it does under a timer, faster than real time, with no wall-clock dependence.
- **Kit:** the drum one-shots, pre-rendered once with `OfflineAudioContext`.
- **Chord tone:** a pitch class in the current chord's bass or pad list.

**Where the evidence comes from.** Every `[O]` below was measured on 2026-11-05 on a throwaway JavaScript prototype of this exact design in the session scratchpad (`t07_audio/`), rendered in cached Chromium 1234 (`chrome.exe` headless) on the RTX 4070 SUPER machine, at 48 kHz unless stated. That prototype is not part of this project and may be gone, so none of it counts as acceptance. Its bass and arp picks used a different hash from `hash01`, its blips, stabs and SFX were only partly built, and it never ran the production engine. The executor runs everything again. `[A]` values are starting points. **Nobody has heard the prototype either.**

Environment facts [O]: in both `chromium-1234` and `chromium_headless_shell-1234`, an `AudioContext` created at page load stays `suspended`; one created inside a real click is `running` at once. `sampleRate` is 48000, `baseLatency` .01. `outputLatency` read 0 inside the click and .04 at 500 ms. `currentTime` advances about 10.67 ms at a time. A real-time `AnalyserNode` tap works headless, with a .5-gain sine reading RMS .353, so a real-time check is possible with no sound device.

**Behaviour of each `AudioEngine` method.** §8.1 carries the short form of this table as a comment.

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `createAudio()` | Creates no audio object. Safe in Node and with no `AudioContext` global |
| `unlock()` | Synchronously in the caller's stack: `new AudioContext({ latencyHint: 'interactive' })` and `resume()`, and nothing else (DI-79, repair D1). The graph, voices, launch sweep and kit go into a queue that `frame` runs after the gesture, one slice per call (see `frame`), so the launch sweep starts on the first frame after the click and music once the kit is in place. Idempotent, so a second call in one gesture (T08 sends `pointerup` then `click`) does nothing unless the state is not `running`, when it calls `resume()` again. Never rejects. Resolves when `running` or after 1000 ms. With no `AudioContext`, or if construction throws, it stays silent and resolves |
| `frame(v)` | While the post-gesture build has work (`work.ts`), it runs one slice and does nothing else. A slice runs its first item, then each next item whose estimate still fits in `SLICE_MS` (2.5 ms): a fill run is sized to about .25 to .6 ms and capped at 8192 samples, a wiring step is estimated at 2 ms, anything else at .5 ms. Alone items, each convolver's buffer set and the first run of the first fill of each kind, start a slice and end it. The first three items compute the two clip curves; wire the master and SFX path, create every node the `AudioGraph` names and queue the kit, then the rest of the graph; and make the voices, sequencer and cue logic and start the launch sweep. The kit's fills, wiring and `OfflineAudioContext` render starts come ahead of the rest of the graph, so its renders start early and run off the main thread, and each returned render's normalising lands after the graph is complete, so music cannot start on a half-built graph. Then a cheap no-op until the context is running and the kit is ready. Then it observes the clock, copies `sim.sync`, `zone.index`, `zone.musicKey`, `sim.mode` and `sim.speed` into scalars, and handles `v.events`. No allocation unless an event fires. Never throws |
| `setVolume(music, sfx)` | Linear gains in `[0, 1]`, clamped; NaN ignored. Callable before `unlock`, and remembered. Applied with `setTargetAtTime` at 15 ms. Default .8 each, though T08 always calls at mount |
| `suspend()` | Stops the timer, stops and disconnects live voices, calls `ctx.suspend()`. Idempotent. Before `unlock` it is remembered |
| `resume()` | `ctx.resume()`, restarts the timer, ramps the master from 0 over 300 ms, and sets `needsAnchor`. The next `frame` re-anchors, and nothing is scheduled until it does, so no burst can play |
| `dispose()` | Clears the timer and listeners, closes the context. Every later call is a no-op |

**Retry (§10.5).** If the context is not `running` when the engine expects it to be, because of iOS `interrupted`, a suspended start or an OS interruption, the engine adds capture-phase listeners on `document` for `pointerup`, `click` and `keydown`. Each calls `ctx.resume()` and removes the listeners once the state is `running`. T08 measured that touch `pointerdown` and Escape do not activate [O: T08 section 10], so `pointerdown` is not listened for and the `keydown` listener returns early on `Escape`. That filter is what A22 tests: once a page has been activated, `resume()` reached `running` from an Escape handler and from a timer with no gesture [O: T07/T08 review, Chromium 1234], so browser policy will not keep it suspended. A suspended start arms these listeners inside `unlock`, before it returns, so the `click` of the same gesture can retry: T10's `launch` does nothing on its second call (T10 A9), so `unlock` is not called again on that path.

**Failure guard.** §3.3's `catch` routes any exception in the frame loop to the fatal page, and silence is a far better outcome than that for an audio fault. The body of `frame`, the timer tick, `setVolume`, `suspend` and `resume` each run in one `try/catch`, which on any exception logs one `console.warn` and disposes the engine. This is not per-note, so it adds no hot-path cost.

### M1. Theory and composer

**Goal:** the entire score is pure data and pure functions, checked as music before a sound exists. **Work:** copy these tables verbatim into `theory.ts`. They were checked by script on the prototype [O]: every melody note on beats 1 and 3 is a chord tone; every note is a chord tone or in the natural-minor scale; melodies span exactly 64 steps with no overlap; no pad voice moves more than 5 semitones between adjacent chords of a progression.

**Key math.** `k = ((musicKey % 12) + 12) % 12`. `padTonic = 52 + (k + 5) % 12`, so A3 at key 0, inside E3 to D#4. `bassTonic = 31 + (k + 2) % 12`, so A1 at key 0, inside G1 to F#2. These fold every key into one register, which is why a circle-of-fifths walk never climbs.

**Blip ladder.** `ladderMidi(chord, key, index)`: the chord's pitch classes, sorted and deduplicated, stacked upward from the first chord tone at or above `padTonic + 12`, for two octaves. `index` is clamped to the top, alternating between the top two after that, so a long chain shimmers rather than drones. If the ladder's top exceeds MIDI 96, the **whole ladder** moves down an octave, never note by note, so it stays monotonic and in the chord (DI-48).

**Result:** `patternFor` writes a bar into caller-owned arrays, with no allocation, for any `bar` up to 2^53 and any `sector`. **Proof:** A1, A1n, with checker functions that take the tables as parameters, so a bad table can be fed to them.

### M2. Clock, sequencer and cue logic

**Goal:** all timing is pure and testable against a fake clock in Node, which is the only way to test an hour.

**`clock.ts`.** `timeOfStep(step) = anchorTime + (step / 4 - anchorBeat) * .6 - comp`, and `drift = timeOf(simBeat) + comp - audioNow`.

| Rule | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Drift filter | EMA with time constant .4 s, using `dt` | `currentTime` moves in 10.67 ms quanta here, and raw `currentTime - performance.now()` spanned 12.1 ms on an idle page while an EMA stayed within .9 ms [O] |
| Slew | `anchorTime` moves toward zero drift at up to 2 ms per second of `dt`, which also reduces the EMA | Inaudible, about .3 ms per step [I]. Absorbs clock skew: 5 ppm measured over 30 s [O], so 100 ppm is a safe test case |
| Re-anchor | EMA above 30 ms, per §7.6 | Filtered so that a coarse audio buffer on another device cannot trigger it |
| Fast re-anchor | Raw drift above 150 ms on 2 consecutive `observe` calls | A dt clamp, a harness tick jump or a stall should not wait for the EMA |
| Latency comp | `clamp(L - .02, 0, .3)`, `L = outputLatency \|\| baseLatency \|\| 0`, read once per bar; applied only if it moved more than 10 ms | Aligns the sound with a displayed frame roughly a frame old [A]. Does not enter `drift`, so it cannot cause a re-anchor |

**`sequencer.ts`.** `tick(now)`: while `timeOfStep(nextStep) < now + windowS` (default .12, per §7.6), schedule that step, then `nextStep += 1`. On reaching a bar line, run the sector reducer, then `patternFor` into the buffer, sampling `sync` there. Scan the bar for notes at this step, up to 96 comparisons, and call the sink. Tick rate is 25 ms, set by the caller; never rAF.

- **Form origin.** On the first start, `formOrigin = floor(firstBar / 4) * 4`, and `patternFor` and `chordAt` receive `bar - formOrigin`. It stays phrase-aligned, because the origin is a multiple of 4, and launch always lands at the start of section A rather than maybe in the break. It is not reset on `resume`.
- **Late policy.** `t >= now`: start at `t`. Up to 30 ms late: start at `now`, count `late`. Later: skip, count `skipped`. A pad chord missed mid-bar is started at `now` for the remainder, so a re-anchor never leaves a bar with no harmony.
- **Never duplicate.** A re-anchor sets `nextStep = max(nextStep, stepAtOrAfter(now))`, so `nextStep` never decreases.
- **Sector reducer.** The first start adopts the current zone at once, with no fill. After that, pending `(index, key)` is whatever `frame` last wrote. Computing bar `b`: if a pending change exists and `(b + 1) % 4 == 0`, `b` becomes the fill bar (`fillNext`). Computing the bar after a fill bar: the pending change activates with `entered = true`, so a crash lands on a phrase line. Worst-case wait 5 bars plus the 120 ms between computing a bar and its downbeat, 12.12 s [arithmetic]; the shortest zone at full boost is 42.7 s [arithmetic]. A second change during the wait replaces the pending one.

**`sfx.ts`.**

- **Chain:** own counter. +1 on a scored `SHARD`. Reset on `HIT`, `GATE_MISS`, `SIGNAL_LOST`, or after 2 bars without a shard. Independent of T02's CHAIN objective.
- **Quantise.** Target the next sixteenth, as §7.6 says. Shards lie on a 12 u lattice and gates on the beat, both on the grid at cruise thanks to T02's beat lock, so an event usually arrives just **after** a grid line, and a plain next-sixteenth would always sound 150 ms late. So: if the previous grid line was at most 30 ms ago [A], start now. Otherwise, the next grid line.
- **Queue.** One blip per step: `step = max(target, lastBlipStep + 1)`. More than 3 steps ahead, the sound is dropped, and the chain still counts.

| Event | Cue | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| `SHARD` | Square + sine an octave up, `ladderMidi(chord, key, max(chain - 1, 0))`, since a cosmetic shard can arrive at chain 0, for example just after `SIGNAL_LOST` reset it, 150 ms pluck, pan `clamp(x / PLAY_HALF_X, -1, 1) * .5` | Quantised |
| `GATE` | Saw stab of ladder notes 0 to 3, 280 ms, through a 3 kHz lowpass | Quantised |
| `GATE_MISS` | Chord root an octave below the ladder, square, sliding down a fourth, 180 ms | Quantised |
| `GRAZE` | Noise through a highpass sweep, 120 ms, panned | Now |
| `HIT` | Kit tom at rate .5, a noise burst, plus the duck | Now |
| `SIGNAL_LOST` | Saw gliding 880 to 55 Hz over 1.2 s, plus noise | Now |
| `GRADE` | `4 - rank` rising ladder notes, one per sixteenth | From the next beat |
| Launch | Saw 110 to 880 Hz over .5 s, low level | Inside `unlock`, needs no kit |

**Result:** the timing logic is proven with no audio. **Proof:** A2 to A4, A7.

### M3. Measuring instruments first

**Goal:** every metric exists, and has been shown able to fail, before there is anything to measure. **Work:** `analysis.ts`, working on `Float32Array`, all pure: `peak`, `clipCount` (samples at `|x| >= .999`), `badCount` (non-finite), `dc`, `rmsDb`, `crestDb`, `corr` (L/R), `monoDb` (mid RMS relative to stereo RMS), `bandShares` (Hann, 8192-point FFT, bands below 250 Hz, 250 to 2 k, 2 to 8 k, above 8 k), `segDb(t0, t1)`, `goertzelDb`, `bassMarginDb(midi)`, `chromaInChord(pcs)`, `onsets`, `maxStepDiff`, `maxAbsDiff`, `spectrogram` into an RGBA array.

`bassMarginDb` is the sum of harmonics 3 to 6 at `midi`, minus the larger of the same sum at `midi ± 1`. **Do not use the fundamental:** an eighth-note gate has sidebands at 3.33 Hz, and a semitone at G1 is 2.9 Hz, so the fundamental test scored -18.7 dB on a correct note [O]. `chromaInChord` is the fraction of 110 to 1800 Hz FFT power in the chord's pitch classes, over a 32768-point Hann window starting .9 s into a bar.

`impulse.ts`: mulberry32 PRNG, no `Math.random`. `fillImpulse(out, sampleRate, tauS, seed, darken)`, **scaled to unit energy.** Unit-amplitude noise with `normalize = false` has about 114x gain, and it pushed the first prototype mix to -7.6 dBFS and correlation .04 [O]. `wav.ts`: 16-bit PCM stereo, with a correct RIFF header.

**Result:** instruments are trusted. **Proof:** A5, A6.

**Graph** (`graph.ts`, any `BaseAudioContext`):

Since repair D1 (D-009) the reverb's convolver is built as mono convolvers per channel, split from a `reverbIn` held at two channels, and each channel's 2.2 s impulse is cut at .75 and 1.5 s into three segments, the later two behind delays of exactly .75 and 1.5 s, merged back. By linearity this is the same convolution; on noise a cut at 1 s read a largest difference of 2.4e-7 at a peak of 2.8 against one stereo convolver, and the channel split alone 0 (section 10). It exists only so that no single buffer set holds the main thread past DI-79's 4 ms.

Compressor: threshold -12, knee 10, ratio 2.5, attack .01, release .18. **It sits before the duck**, or it would give the duck back. The 24 Hz highpass removes the kick's DC: .005 before, under .0001 after [O]. Soft-clip curve: 4097 points over `u` in `[-2, 2]`, `y = u` up to .7, above that `.7 + .28 * tanh((|u| - .7) / .28)`. That alone did not bound the output: the 2x oversampling rings when driven hard, 1.15 at +12 dB on the real graph [O: A9c first run]. A second waveshaper, identity up to .97 with a tight knee to .982 and no oversampling, does, and at normal levels it does nothing (Decision log).

Putting the pump in each chord's own envelope means **no long-lived `AudioParam` carries per-kick automation**, so R1's forever has no growing event list there. Short notes get their pump through velocity.

**Duck and modes** (`graph.ts`), using only `cancelScheduledValues(t)` and `setTargetAtTime`, which start from the current value by definition. So nothing reads `.value`, and a re-hit in mid-duck cannot jump.

| State | `duckFilter` | `duckGain` |
|---|---|---|
| DEMO, PLAY | 20 kHz | 1 |
| CRUISE | 2.4 kHz | .708, -3 dB |
| `SIGNAL_LOST` | 350 Hz | .5, -6 dB |
| Hit, for 400 ms over any of those | 500 Hz | base x .3548, -9 dB |

Hit envelope: target at `t` with tau 5 ms; back to base at `t + .4` with tau 40 ms for gain and 50 ms for frequency. Measured [O] against the same render with no hit: below 150 Hz -8.8 dB, 2 k and up -37.7 dB, after recovery 0.0 dB in all bands, and no new click (attack .0354 vs .0401 base; release .1635 vs .1547).

**Polyphony.** Cap `MAX_LIVE_SOURCES = 96` [A], counted by `onended`. `suspend()` empties the live list and zeroes the count directly, because `onended` does not fire while the context is suspended and does fire on resume for voices already stopped [O: T07/T08 review: 0 after 1 s suspended, 3 of 3 after resume]. So A21's `live voices 0` cannot come from `onended`, and a late `onended` from a cleared voice must not decrement. Peak at T4 was 68 [O]. At the cap, drop in this order: blips and gate stabs (admitted only under `cap - 8`), hats (under `cap - 4`), arp (under `cap`). Stabs joined the blips in repair R1 (Decision log). Kick, snare, bass, pad and lead are never dropped, so a cap can thin the texture but not break the song. Nor are the one-per-event cues: miss (1 source), graze (1), hit (2), lost (2), and the launch sweep (1, once per unlock). T02's event buffer holds 64 events a frame, so the worst frame those can add is 64 hits, 128 sources over the cap; real play has a hit every few seconds at most. That bound is accepted as §7.6's "cap polyphony" (V1 ruling, Decision log). Clamp every automation time to at least `currentTime`, and use .0001 rather than 0 as an exponential ramp's floor.

**Offline driver** (`offline.ts`). Builds a real graph, kit, voices and sequencer in an `OfflineAudioContext`, registers `suspend(k * tickS)` for all k, and in each callback calls `sequencer.tick(ctx.currentTime)`, fires scene events, then `resume()`. Default `tickS` .025; the cost is negligible at 207 suspends in 9 ms [O]. `layers` masks at the sink, so a solo is that layer's voices through the real buses. `faults` exist only here and are the negative controls for section 7.

Do not schedule a whole render up front: every not-yet-started node is still pulled, which took 6 to 18 s for 39 s of audio, against 3 to 7.6 s scheduled incrementally [O].

**Dev page.** `dev/audio.html`: a **Start** button fixed at `left 16, top 16, 240 x 64`, so a test clicks by coordinates; SYNC 1 to 16; sector 0 to 5; key 0 to 11; mode; Hit, Shard, Gate, Miss, Graze, Grade buttons; music and sfx sliders; a "Render" button with WAV download; a canvas. The page script runs synchronously to completion, so it is ready when `goto` returns, and no polling happens before the gesture (T08's trap). Query: `?eager=1` makes a context at load, as the recorder's control. `window.__audio` exposes `scene(o)` (returns metrics), `shot(o)`, `live` (engine, stats, tap) and `allocProbe(n)`.

**Result:** a render exists and every gate can run. **Proof:** A8 to A18.

## 8. Idempotence and recovery

- Safe to re-run: every check and test. `listen:` overwrites only `test-results/T07-listen/`, and `T07_SOAK` is off by default.

## 10. Surprises and discoveries

Entries from the 2026-11-05 prototype on Chromium 1234 ran on no project code. Entries marked execution ran on this project's code, on Chromium 1234 and this machine, while sibling tasks shared it.

- **Offline renders are not byte-identical,** so a golden-bytes test would flake. Two identical mix renders differed in 944,190 of 969,600 samples, by at most 1.2e-4. Each node type alone gave 0 differences (oscillator, biquad, convolver, compressor, delay feedback, chorus, waveshaper). 40 oscillators into one gain differed by up to 2.4e-7, in a different pattern each run. Solo bass, whose notes rarely overlap, gave 0. **Likely** a varying summing order at a node with many inputs [I; the source was not read]. Effects off made no difference. Hence the tolerance in A17.
- **The first mix was -7.6 dBFS with L/R correlation .04.** Cause: a unit-amplitude noise impulse under `normalize = false` has about 114x gain [arithmetic], so reverb dominated everything. Unit-energy scaling took RMS to -11.8 and correlation to .94. The balance then came from the solo measurements.
- **A correct bass note can score -18.7 dB at its own fundamental.** See M3. Harmonics 3 to 6 scored 5.1 to 29.6 dB on right notes, and -10.7 to -22.5 on wrong ones.
- **Scheduling a whole offline render up front is 2 to 3 times slower,** since unstarted nodes are still pulled. `onended` also cannot measure polyphony there: it reported every node live. Polyphony is measured in real time only.
- **A compressor after the duck would undo the duck,** by raising gain as its input falls. It sits before.
- **`cancelScheduledValues` does not prune.** `setValueAtTime(.5, 1); setValueAtTime(.2, 2); cancelScheduledValues(1.5)` read 1, .5, .5 at .5 s, 1.5 s, 2.5 s, so only future events go. `Agents.md` states this (DI-29). Separately, Chromium showed no cost from history: 30 s of render took 10 ms after 0 past events, 11 ms after 1e4 and 1e5. Other browsers were not tested.
- **`new AudioContext` cost 52.5 ms in the click handler,** the first time in the process. Impulse fill 11.7 ms, graph 6.4 ms, kit ready 190 ms after the click. It is a one-time hitch at launch, inside the title transition.
- **Real-time run, T4, idle page, 30 s** [O]: 1,299 sources created, peak 68 live, 29 at the end. Tick p50 0, p99 .6, max 2.9 ms. Heaviest step 2.4 ms, a pad chord. Lead at scheduling .100 to .112 s. No missed step.
- **Clock skew here is small:** `currentTime` against `performance.now()` moved .16 ms in 30 s, about 5 ppm. One machine, one sample. Raw difference spanned 12.1 ms from buffering.
- **A module-level `let` holding a large double allocates.** In a tight loop under Vitest it caused 12 scavenges per 3e6 calls, against 0 to 1 for the same arithmetic into a `Float64Array` and an object field. So hot-path state lives in typed arrays or object fields. A `PerformanceObserver` on `gc` separates the two.
- **Reading `.value` during automation was correct in Chromium** (.1353 for an expected e^-2). The plan avoids depending on this, because support in other browsers is unverified [M].
- **2026-11-05 execution: the production render reproduced the prototype's numbers.** Snare 8.2 and 43.7 dB (prototype the same), tail -44.3 dB (same), duck -8.8 and -38.0 (-8.8, -37.7), solos within .6 dB by `layers`, peak sources 68 (same). The plan's starting values needed no retune.
- **A one-channel pump reading is mostly beating.** With the pump off, RMS before a kick minus RMS after read 0.9, 2.9, 0.8 dB on L, so A13's literal control failed at step 8, on a correct render. Detuned saws move +-3 dB across 50 ms in each channel (power curve in 50 ms hops: -17 to -25 dB with no pump). The prototype's "3.7 to 5.8" was partly this: the same render now reads 3.8, 7.2, 5.0 single-render, and 3.75, 3.79, 3.89 once the no-pump render is subtracted. The renders are equal to within 1e-4, so the subtraction leaves the envelope alone.
- **That reading also exposed a real bug of mine.** The first pad envelope recovered from a downbeat kick at the slope of a full 320 ms ramp but stopped at 320 ms, so it held at .847 until the next kick. Step 4 read 2.05 dB, and the power curve showed the pumped render 1.5 dB under the flat one at 500 ms. Fixed. The prototype had a different bug here, which M4's table inherited: T0's kicks at steps 6 and 8 are closer than the recovery, so events sorted in time crossed ramps and jumped .55 to 1 in 8 ms. A recovery now stops at the next kick at the value it had reached.
- **The soft clip's oversampling rings.** +12 dB read peak 1.15 through the soft clip alone. See M4.
- **Every sound leaves the graph late, by a constant.** Blips read 3.0 to 3.5 ms at a .5 ms hop, and 0.0 ms with no clip stage, so that is the 2x oversampling [I]. Kicks read 8 ms, the same plus the compressor's look-ahead [M: about 6 ms in Chromium's compressor, source not read]. Constant, far under §7.6's 30 ms, and SFX lead music by about 5 ms. T10 and H11 inherit it.
- **`ctx.currentTime` boxes a double.** 1e5 reads cost 1195 to 1292 KB by the sampling profiler, 12 B a read, against 5.8 to 7.3 KB for 1e5 no-event `frame` calls. See Decision log.
- **Real frames allocate far more while cold than the one-task loop suggests.** `frame` in real frames read 14 KB/s in its first seconds (`observe` 9.9 KB/s, `updateWind` 1.5, `frame` 1.0, `exp` and `abs` .9 each) and 1.1 KB/s after 90 s of play. At 40 observes a second, `observe` is not hot enough to optimise at once, and baseline code boxes each double. So at 60 Hz the first tens of seconds run near 240 B per frame, over §9.2's 128 B line, and then well under it. T09's gate does not see it, because audio is inert under the harness (seam).
- **A computed double passed to a call that does not inline is boxed by the caller,** the argument-side twin of `Agents.md`'s note on returned doubles. `observe` with computed `t` and `beat`: 23 scavenges over 3e6 calls, 0 with literal arguments. `patternFor` with `2 ** 40 + i`: 23 to 47, with `i`: 0 to 2. `push(out, ..., len, vel)` as a helper: 15. Hence `claim`, which takes no doubles.
- **A 2 ms envelope follows single cycles of a 50 Hz kick,** so a kick-only render read 15 onsets instead of 8, retriggering on its own zero crossings. `onsets` gained `minGapS`.
- **A scene ran on into its own tail.** The sequencer kept ticking past `bars`, so the 1-bar reverb test read -23 dB (the next bar) and `noReverb` no quieter, and A14 read a ninth kick. The driver now stops at the scene's end.
- **The first step could be late by a timer period.** A20 read `late: 1` on one first run: `restart` picks a step at or after now, and the timer may not come for 25 ms. The anchoring frame now ticks once with the same `now`. 0 since, on 3 repeats and every later run.
- **Every saw of a chord starts in phase.** So each chord's beating repeats identically from its own downbeat. It is within M4's recipe, and only ears can say if it is a fault: see H3. Staggering each source's start by up to one period would be a two-line change in `padChord`.
- **The form makes silence by design.** `arp` and `lead` solo below their SYNC thresholds rendered exact digital silence, so a -90 dB gate cannot pass by accident there.
- **Repair R1: the recorder never counted a buffer source.** In Chromium, `AudioBufferSourceNode.prototype` declares its own `start`, so wrapping `AudioScheduledSourceNode.prototype.start` missed every kit one-shot and noise burst. On the live voices the wrap read kick 0, hat 0, tom 0, against bass 3, arp 2, lead 3 and pad 4 per voice; with both wrapped, 1, 1, 1.
